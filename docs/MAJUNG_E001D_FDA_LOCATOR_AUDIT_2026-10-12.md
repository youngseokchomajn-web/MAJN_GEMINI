## Interpretation audit

1. **Effectiveness and safety support remain separate.** FDA’s summary states that the 2017 study’s endpoints were not suitable to establish effectiveness for the intended indication, while its thermoregulation, oxygenation and adverse-event observations contributed to safety support.
2. **The 2023 overall randomized comparison and subgroup analyses remain separate.** The 2023 RCT’s overall treatment-rate difference was not statistically significant; favorable duration/dose findings in the responder subgroup are not equivalent to a positive overall primary result.
3. **“No adverse events observed” is bounded to the submitted studies and their observation conditions.** It does not establish zero risk for all infants or long-term use.
4. **The De Novo decision is indication-specific.** It cannot be used as evidence that MAJUNG is effective or safe for general infant soothing or sleep.

## Audit result

- Verified the 9-page PDF and printed-page locators for intended use, bench testing, clinical evidence, risks, special controls and benefit-risk conclusion.
- Confirmed that FDA’s effectiveness limitation and safety-support statement for the 2017 study appear together on printed p.7.
